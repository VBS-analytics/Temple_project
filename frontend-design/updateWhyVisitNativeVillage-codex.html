Blessings of MahaPeriyava & Ramana Maharishi

Two sacred vijayams that sanctified Kakkalani Village

Kanchi Shri Mahaperiyava's vijayam to our village was around the year 1920. He visited the Pichu Iyer family during the upanayanam of Shri Mahalingam (grandfather of Dr. Sabesan), stayed in the house, and performed pooja.

Details about this event were shared in an audio by Dr. Sabesan (son of late Mr. Swaminathan alias Balu, Psychology Professor in Annamalai University, and grandson of Mr. Mahalingam).

Audio Reference — Dr. Sabesan

Kanchi Shri Mahaperiyava's second vijayam to our village was through the Pannai family in 1954. While elaborate details are not available, Shri R. Vaithyanathan (the senior-most available member from the Pannai family) shared what he could recollect.

During this vijayam, Shri Mahaperiyava stayed for a few days on the plot next to Mangalam Periamma's house, where special hut-type arrangements were made.

The location is referenced on the website home page.

Mr M Ganapathy (Mahadeva Iyer Ganapati)1903 – 1976

Padma Bhushan 1954

Mahadeva Iyer Ganapati (known as M. Ganapati) was an eminent Indian engineer renowned for his leadership in major national infrastructure projects. The Indian government awarded him the inaugural Padma Bhushan in 1954. He served as President of the Institution of Engineers (India) for 1973–74.

Mr Arunachala Srinivasan

Padma Bhushan 1974

Mr Arunachala Srinivasan was awarded the prestigious Padma Bhushan in 1974, recognising his outstanding contribution and dedicated service to the nation.

Mr Arunachala Sundaresan

Estate Manager

Mr Sundaresan was a genius in mathematics and renowned for his exceptional sharp memory. Among his distinguished service, he served as the "Vadabathimangalam Estate Manager" at Thiruvarur.

Vadabathimangalam estate was one of the largest in Thiruvarur, owned by Mr Thyagaraja Mudaliar — an ardent devotee of Lord Thyagaraja Swamy. Periodically, he would review his assets and compare their value with those of the Thyagaraja Swamy temple. Whatever excess he found, he would immediately transfer to the temple trust.

"His unwavering principle: his own assets should never exceed those of the temple."

To manage such an estate with such values, Mr Thyagaraja Mudaliar found in Mr Arunachalam Sundaresan the most able person for the role.

Mr Vaidyanathan GanesanGrandson of Mr Arunachalam

Mahavir Chakra 1960

Mr Vaidyanathan Ganesan made a supreme sacrifice and was awarded the prestigious Peacetime Mahavir Chakra in 1960 — one of India's highest military honours.

Importance of Visiting Our Village

Our Sacred Duty

Importance of Visit and Offer Service to Our Village Deities

Just as we must never forget the sacrifices and efforts of our parents, who shaped our lives and guided us to where we are today, we must also remember our duty towards our Kuladeivam (Family deity) and Grama Devathas (Village deities). They have an important place in Hinduism and are like our local guardians and protectors. Hence, worshipping them is vital for one's good life. Such worship and traditions must be inculcated in our children and future generations for their benefit.

Village Significance

Kakkalani — Its Name and Religious Significance

Our village Kakkalani is located within 10 km from the famous Thiruvarur Thyagaraja Swami Temple, where Lord Shiva showers his blessings in the form of a Maragadha Lingam worshipped by Lord Indra himself. Nithya pooja is offered on all four kalams every day.

One of our generous donors and village members, Shri R. Srinivasan (Vasu), was blessed with an opportunity to offer physical service at the place where daily poojas are performed to this Lingam.

Our village is blessed to be in close proximity to this temple, enjoying its positive spiritual vibrations.

Divine Grace

Blessings of Great Souls

Shri Kanchi Mahaperiyava came to this village twice and visited all three temples, sanctifying them with his divine presence. Both the Pichu Iyer family and the Pannai Family are blessed — and through them, the entire village is blessed by Mahaperiyava.

A member of the Pannai family was blessed with the opportunity to stay at the Ashram of Shri Ramana Maharishi and render service to him. He was later brought back and stayed in the village till his last breath. During Mahaperiyava's second visit, he acknowledged this person as apart from all others — in a siddha state due to his association with Shri Ramana Maharishi.

Shri Arunachala Sastrigal, a renowned Sanskrit scholar and Vedic pandit from our village, was well recognised by His Holiness Kanchi Mahaperiyava.

Our Responsibilities

Why We Must Visit & Serve

Many eminent personalities with accolades have come from this small village. All these achievements were possible because of Eashwara Krupai & village deities' blessings.
When we stand before the deities in our village temples, we also receive the blessings of the great souls who once worshipped there.
It is our duty to share this sacred knowledge with our families and the younger generation, and to ensure that daily poojas and Naivedhyam continue in our village temples by actively participating in these efforts.
For many families, the village deity also serves as their Kuladeivam, strengthening their spiritual bond with the village.
If all the village temples are looked after properly, the village too will become prosperous.

"Let us take this opportunity to visit our temples, offer our service, and seek the blessings of our village deities. By doing so, we preserve our traditions, honour our ancestors, and pass on this sacred heritage to our future generations."

"கோவில் விளங்கக் குடி விளங்கும்"

பல முயற்சிகள், தியாகங்கள் செய்து நம்மை வளர்த்து ஆளாக்கி இன்று இந்த உயர்ந்த நிலையில் நாம் இருப்பதற்கு காரணமான நமது பெற்றோரை அவர்களின் வயோதிக காலத்தில் பேணி காக்க நாம் எப்படி தவறக்கூடாதோ அது போல நமது குல தெய்வங்களையும், கிராம தெய்வங்களையும் வழிபடுவதையும், சேவை செய்வதையும் மறந்து விடக்கூடாது. சனாதன தர்மத்தின்படி அவர்கள் நம்மை காப்பாற்றும் உள்ளூர் பாதுகாவலராகவே கருதப்படுகிறார்கள். எனவே நமது நல்வாழ்வுக்கு அவர்களை வழிபடுவது சால சிறந்தது.

இங்கே நமது கிராமமான கக்கழனி இந்த பெயரையும், புகழையும் எவ்வாறு பெற்றது என்ற வரலாற்று சிறப்பை முதலில் காண்போம்.

இந்த கிராமம், பஞ்ச பூத ஸ்தலங்களில் பிருத்வி ஸ்தலமாகவும், தேவார பாடல்கள் பாடபெற்ற உலக பிரசித்தி பெற்ற மரகதலிங்கமாக இருந்து, இந்தரனாலேயே பூஜிக்கப்பட்டதுமான சிவனார் கோயில் கொண்டுள்ள திருவாரூர் தியாகராஜ கோயிலிலிருந்து 10 கிலோ மீட்டர் தொலைவில் உள்ளது. இந்த சிவனுக்கு தினமும் நாலு கால பூஜையும் நைவேத்யத்துடன் சிறப்பாக நடைபெறுகிறது. நமது கிராமம் இந்த புகழ் பெற்ற சிவ கோயிலின் அருகாமையில் அமைய பெற்றதால் இங்கும் அத்தனை நேர்மறை ஆன்மீக அதிர்வுகள் அமைய பெற்ற இடமாக உள்ளது.

பல்வேறு ஆவணங்கள் பதிவுகள், அங்குள்ள மூத்த தலைவர்கள், மற்றும் மக்களுடன் நடத்திய உரையாடல்கள் மூலமாக நாம் அறிய வந்த விஷயங்கள்: பல புகழுக்கு சொந்தக்காரர்களும் விருதுகளை வென்றவர்களும் இந்த மண்ணின் மைந்தர்கள்.
