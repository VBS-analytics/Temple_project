Responsive behaviour notes
• Design A (Split Panel) — left panel hides on phones (<768 px); a compact gradient header bar replaces it. On iPad the split panel reappears.
• Design B (Dark Frosted) — card stretches to full width with reduced padding; background dark gradient fills the whole screen on all sizes.
• Design C (Clean Minimal) — card stretches to full width with reduced padding; warm cream background fills the whole screen.
• Design D (Image Background ✨) — village map image stays FULLY VISIBLE on all screen sizes; semi-transparent card sits centered on top; deity images scroll at the bottom exactly like the current page.
• All inputs, buttons, and labels scale fluidly — no horizontal scroll on any listed device.
