Why We Should Visit Our Village

Around 1920

First Vijayam to Kakkalani

Kanchi Shri Mahaperiyava's vijayam to our village was around the year 1920. He visited the Pichu Iyer family during the upanayanam of Shri Mahalingam (grandfather of Dr. Sabesan), stayed in the house, and performed pooja.

Details about this event were shared in an audio by Dr. Sabesan (son of late Mr. Swaminathan alias Balu, Psychology Professor in Annamalai University, and grandson of Mr. Mahalingam).

🔊 Audio Reference — Dr. Sabesan
1954

Second Vijayam — Through the Pannai Family

Kanchi Shri Mahaperiyava's second vijayam to our village was through the Pannai family in 1954. While elaborate details are not available, Shri R. Vaithyanathan (the senior-most available member from the Pannai family) shared what he could recollect.

During this vijayam, Shri Mahaperiyava stayed for a few days on the plot next to Mangalam Periamma's house, where special hut-type arrangements were made.

The location of this visit is referenced on the website home page.

Distinguished Service

Mr Arunachala Sundaresan — Vadabathimangalam Estate Manager

Mr Sundaresan was a genius in mathematics and renowned for his exceptional sharp memory. Among his distinguished service, he served as the "Vadabathimangalam Estate Manager" at Thiruvarur.

Vadabathimangalam estate was one of the largest in Thiruvarur, owned by Mr Thyagaraja Mudaliar — an ardent devotee of Lord Thyagaraja Swamy. Periodically, he reviewed his assets and compared their value with those of the Thyagaraja Swamy temple. Whatever excess he found, he immediately transferred to the temple trust.

"His unwavering principle: his own assets should never exceed those of the temple."

To manage such an estate with such values, Mr Thyagaraja Mudaliar found in Mr Arunachalam Sundaresan the most able person for the role.

Mahavir Chakra 1960

Mr Vaidyanathan Ganesan

Grandson of Mr Arunachalam, Mr Vaidyanathan Ganesan made a supreme sacrifice and was awarded the prestigious Peacetime Mahavir Chakra in 1960 — one of India's highest military honours.

Our Sacred Duty

Importance of Visit and Offer Service to Our Village Deities

Just as we must never forget the sacrifices and efforts of our parents, who shaped our lives and guided us to where we are today, we must also remember our duty towards our Kuladeivam (Family deity) and Grama Devathas (Village deities). They have an important place in Hinduism and are like our local guardians and protectors. Hence, worshipping them is vital for one's good life.

Such worship and traditions must be inculcated in our children and future generations for their benefit.

Village Significance

Kakkalani — Its Name and Religious Significance

Our village Kakkalani is located within 10 km from the famous Thiruvarur Thyagaraja Swami Temple, where Lord Shiva showers his blessings in the form of a Maragadha Lingam worshipped by Lord Indra himself. Nithya pooja is offered on all four kalams every day.

One of our generous donors and village members, Shri R. Srinivasan (Vasu), was blessed with an opportunity to offer physical service at the place where daily poojas are performed to this Lingam.

Our village is blessed to be in close proximity to this temple, enjoying its positive spiritual vibrations.

Divine Grace

Blessings of Great Souls

Shri Kanchi Mahaperiyava came to this village twice and visited all three temples, sanctifying them with his divine presence. Both the Pichu Iyer family and the Pannai Family are blessed — and through them, the entire village is blessed by Mahaperiyava.

A member of the Pannai family was blessed with the opportunity to stay at the Ashram of Shri Ramana Maharishi and render service to him. He was later brought back and stayed in the village till his last breath.

"Shri Kanchi Mahaperiyava, during his second visit, acknowledged this person as apart from all others — in a siddha state due to his association with Shri Ramana Maharishi."

Shri Arunachala Sastrigal, a renowned Sanskrit scholar and Vedic pandit from our village, was well recognised by His Holiness Kanchi Mahaperiyava.

Our Responsibilities

Why We Must Visit & Serve

  • Many eminent personalities with accolades have come from this small village. All these achievements were possible because of Eashwara Krupai & village deities' blessings.
  • When we stand before the deities in our village temples, we also receive the blessings of the great souls who once worshipped there.
  • It is our duty to share this sacred knowledge with our families and the younger generation, and to ensure that daily poojas and Naivedhyam continue in our village temples.
  • For many families, the village deity also serves as their Kuladeivam, strengthening their spiritual bond with the village.
  • If all the village temples are looked after properly, the village too will become prosperous.

"Let us take this opportunity to visit our temples, offer our service, and seek the blessings of our village deities. By doing so, we preserve our traditions, honour our ancestors, and pass on this sacred heritage to our future generations."
